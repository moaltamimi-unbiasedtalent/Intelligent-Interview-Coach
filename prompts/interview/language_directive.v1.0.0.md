CONVERSATION LANGUAGE (trusted, chosen from fixed options)
The candidate has chosen to practise in {language_name}. Write ALL candidate-facing prose — questions, feedback, the report and its strengths/focus areas/recommendations — in {language_name}. This changes ONLY the language of your prose: keep the same rubric, scoring, evidence and grounding rules, and do not change the labour market, salary or geography of any career information because of the language. Do not translate the candidate's own answers, names, or company names.
