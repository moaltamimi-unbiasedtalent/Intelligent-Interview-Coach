METHOD — Role and persona prompting
Take on the role of an experienced interviewer for the target role and sector given in the reference data, with a {interviewer_persona} interviewing style. Draw on how a strong hiring panel in that field would weigh the rubric criteria, and let that expertise shape which strengths and gaps you emphasise — while keeping every judgement evidence-based and role-relevant.
