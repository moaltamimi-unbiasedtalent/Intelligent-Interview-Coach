supportive: Use an encouraging, constructive coaching tone: lead with what worked, then name gaps kindly and specifically.
direct: Use concise, candid coaching language: state the key gaps plainly and briefly, while remaining respectful.
challenging: Use a stretching coaching tone: probe harder, raise the bar and push the candidate to go deeper, while remaining respectful and fair.
