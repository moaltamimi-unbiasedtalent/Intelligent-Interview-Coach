# Pilot 2 anonymised participant summaries (RC-P10-003)

Only the three sessions with substantially complete structured evidence are summarised. **No summary exists for P01, P03 or P05**: their structured task and rating evidence was mostly blank and nothing has been inferred. No names, emails, CV details, transcripts, recordings or screenshots are included.

## P02
Italian interface, English Mo conversation, MacBook Pro, Safari, voice tested, executive career stage, AI experience 4/5. Mean rating 3.83.
- Created an Opportunity unaided; the "one job in one place" idea made sense (4).
- Rated Mo guidance 3, score clarity 3, Sources confidence 4, privacy confidence 4, overall trust 4.
- Did not find the sharing and revoke controls.
- Named the distinction between AI-generated, externally sourced, candidate and verified content as the most confusing part (P2-04).

## P04
English interface and conversation, MacBook Pro, Safari, voice tested, experienced HR / onboarding professional, AI experience 1/5. Mean rating 4.75.
- Highest ratings of the three: 5 on getting started, navigation, Opportunity clarity, Mo guidance, Practice usefulness, feedback clarity, overall trust and likelihood to use or recommend.
- Rated Sources confidence 4, privacy confidence 4, visual quality 4.
- Did not find the sharing and revoke controls.
