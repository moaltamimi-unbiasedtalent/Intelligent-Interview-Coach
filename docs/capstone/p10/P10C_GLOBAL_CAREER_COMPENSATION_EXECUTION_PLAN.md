**Design (provisional, to be reconciled with the unrecoverable GKB set):** one canonical occupation identity (reuse `CanonicalOccupation`) holding, per classification scheme, a versioned `ClassificationRef` (scheme, code, edition, publisher, retrieval date, source checksum); crosswalk records reuse `OccupationCrosswalk` with `MappingType` and `MappingStrength` plus an explicit **mapping status** of exactly five values: `verified` (an official, published crosswalk row), `partial` (one-to-many or broader/narrower official relationship), `ambiguous` (several plausible targets and no official arbiter), `unsupported` (no mapping exists for that scheme pair or geography), `unknown` (not yet assessed). No crosswalk row may be produced without a published source or a recorded curated rationale (`CURATED_MANUAL`, never `OFFICIAL_MAPPING`). Language aliases carry `language` and provenance; an alias never creates a crosswalk. A `SourceOccupation` entity records the occupation exactly as the source publishes it (code, title, edition) so a source row is never silently re-keyed.

## 5. Compensation V2: provisional architecture
Current V1 (`CompensationRecord`): source id, occupation code, empty or free-text title, free-string geography, country, region, industry, year, currency, pay period (annual, monthly, hourly; **weekly exists in data but is not in the documented set**), statistic type (median, mean, percentile), value and bounds, sample quality, source URL. It guards against mixing currencies and periods.
**Proposed `CompensationRecordV2` (an architecture proposal; the exact required form is UNVERIFIED because the original specification is unrecovered):**
- Identity: `record_id`, `source_occupation` (code, scheme, edition, source-published title) and a nullable link to the canonical occupation with a mapping status (section 3); an occupation is never inferred from title text alone.
- Place: structured ISO-3166 country, optional region (NUTS or equivalent) and an explicit `coverage_scope` (national, regional, supranational).
- Money: `currency` (ISO-4217, validated), `pay_period` (hour, day, week, month, year; raw as published), `basis` (gross or net, and employer-cost where published; unknown stays unknown), `statistic` (median, mean, percentile with the percentile value), `value`, optional `lower` and `upper` with their definition, `sample_size` where the source publishes it.
- Time: `reference_period`, `publication_date`, `data_vintage` (edition), `retrieved_at`, `freshness_class` (derived, never edited by hand).
- Provenance: `source_id`, `dataset`, `publisher`, `source_url`, `licence`, `redistribution_allowed`, `normalization` (list of transformations with rationale; a derived annualization from weekly pay is a labelled transformation, never silent), `origin` (official_local, authorised_manual, synthetic_fixture; fixtures can never be served as evidence).
- Honesty: `confidence_basis` (a provenance kind, not an invented score), `coverage_limitations`, `abstention_reason` where a lookup returned nothing.
Integrity rules carried from the approved targets: every reported figure has a citation; no figure is generated or estimated by a model; an unsupported market, unresolved or ambiguous occupation, stale vintage, currency or period that cannot be reconciled, or a synthetic-fixture origin yields explicit abstention with the reason; US BLS or UK ONS data never stands in for another country; gross versus net is never assumed.
**Source precedence:** the existing authority levels and `COUNTRY_SOURCE_PRIORITY` remain the starting point; the proposal moves the table to data (per country and per statistic type, with edition and precedence in a governed registry) and keeps official over public framework over industry, same-country over supranational, and fixtures excluded. It must not contradict the unrecovered C1-C4 contract; D-02 asks the owner to supply it before wave E.

## 6. Source governance requirements
Per source the registry must record: publisher, dataset, geography, coverage, licence and redistribution, update frequency, access mechanism (local manual, official download, official API, authorised API), reliability class, precedence, applicable taxonomy, edition and checksum. Classes stay distinct: official public dataset, licensed dataset, employer-provided data, candidate-provided information, AI-generated guidance. Existing manifest fields already cover most of this (`authority`, `licence`, `licence_review_required`, `acquisition_type`, `refresh_frequency`, `storage_target`); gaps: licence text is blank for the ESCO family, ISCO-08, KldB, Berufenet and the Cedefop sets; NOC, SINCO, CBO, ILOSTAT, UK SOC 2020 and national statistics offices are absent; no per-source edition or checksum is bound to the runtime stores (the normalized build metadata holds checksums for 12 sources). No unapproved source is to be ingested and no licence restriction bypassed; LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Indeed and Levels.fyi scraping stays forbidden.

## 7. Product-coverage gap analysis (the 26 unhandled cases)
Measured at P5-B: `insufficient_evidence` 93.7% (386 of 412 handled), gate 95% (at most 20 unhandled). This wave inspected every one of the 26 against the local stores. **Finding: official data exists locally for 13 of the 26; the failures are occupation-graph wiring defects, not absent data.**
- **Root cause (verified in the stores and the retrieval code):** compensation rows are keyed by SOC (OEWS) or SOC 2020 (ASHE) **code with an empty occupation title**, while `_role_or_comp_compensation` filters **only by title**; the resolver returns ESCO/O*NET candidates whose native SOC code is not stored. Hence a join never happens. The same pattern affects the labour-market tables (keyed by title strings; BLS wording such as "Financial and investment analysts" does not match the resolver's "Financial Analysts") and cross-source attributes (education and outlook attributes exist only for BLS occupations, but the resolver's chosen candidates come from other sources).
- **Class W (wiring, official data present, 13 cases):** US compensation for Software Developer (OEWS 15-1252), Accountant (13-2011), Financial Analyst (13-2051), Human Resources Manager (11-3121), Sales Manager (11-2022); UK compensation for the same five occupations (ASHE publishes unit-group rows keyed by SOC 2020, but no mapping from ESCO, ISCO or O*NET to SOC 2020 exists locally; which unit groups correspond is deliberately not decided here and the mapping must be acquired from a published source, see D-04); education for Civil Engineer (BLS OOH "Civil Engineers" exists); future growth and annual openings for Financial Analysts (BLS projections "Financial and investment analysts"). Expected evidence: sources `bls_oews`, `ons_ashe`, `bls_ooh` and `bls_projections` respectively.
- **Class A (ambiguous or no single official occupation, 13 cases):** Data Analyst and Product Manager compensation (US and UK, 4); education for Data Analyst, Product Manager and Project Manager (3); future growth for Data Analysts and Product Managers (2); short-term outlook for Data Analysts and Product Managers (2); annual openings for Data Analysts and Product Managers (2). Official classifications have no single occupation for these titles (Data Analyst maps to several, such as data scientists or management analysts; Product Manager has no SOC occupation). The correct behaviour is an explicit **ambiguous mapping** outcome with disclosure of the candidate official occupations, or abstention; **not** a silently chosen mapping. The evaluator currently expects evidence for them, so the expectation itself needs an owner decision (D-05).
- **Attribution correction (unverified in the original classification):** P5-B recorded 14 compensation cases and "12 education and career outlook" cases as depending on P10D. This evidence shows all 26 sit in P10C scope (occupation graph, crosswalks, ambiguity handling and wiring); none was shown to need multilingual knowledge. The original classification is preserved here as reported and **not silently reassigned**: the owner is asked to confirm the re-attribution (D-06). Counting arithmetic: reaching 95% needs at least 6 of the 26 handled; Class W alone would close 13.
- No threshold is changed, no synthetic salary is counted as evidence, and the existing evaluation artifacts were not modified.

## 8. CI-reproducible evaluation fixture (design, not implemented)
Problem: `eval_product_coverage` reads gitignored stores built locally (built 2026-09-12 at commit 8522dc5), writes its report to a temporary directory, and the committed `evaluations/product_coverage/summary.md` (97%, built 2026-08-29) is stale. Design: a committed, versioned fixture set `tests/fixtures/knowledge_v1/` containing (a) a minimal roles/compensation/labour/education store **built from real official rows only** under licences that permit redistribution (BLS public domain; ONS under OGL v3.0 with attribution; O*NET CC BY 4.0 with attribution) for the 412-case occupations, (b) the exact crosswalk rows used, with `source`, `edition`, `retrieved_at`, `licence` and checksum in a metadata file, (c) the labelled cases, and (d) a loader that builds temporary SQLite stores so the evaluator never opens developer stores; the report states `evidence: fixture` versus `live/local build`, mock and live evidence are never merged, and fixture rows are never synthetic salaries. A separate scheduled job may compare the fixture to a local full build. No sensitive candidate data is involved. Existing artifacts are not overwritten; the new evaluator writes under its own directory.

## 9. Product integration (reuse, do not duplicate)
Career AI and occupation discovery consume the occupation graph through the existing resolver and router (extension points: a graph-backed resolver, data-driven precedence registry). Career pathways reuse transitions and relationships already normalised. Opportunity workflows and Preparation Packs consume compensation and occupation evidence only through the Pack's explicit optional boundary (`compensation_not_included_p10c` becomes a conditional section; a Pack never computes a salary). Company intelligence is unchanged. The candidate profile's `career_geography` and the opportunity country select the geography; interface and conversation languages never do. Admin governance: W10.8 knowledge administration and the Admin source registry are the operating surface; P10C supplies the data model, not new Admin screens (W12.4F owns Admin completeness). No competing career or compensation engine is created.
