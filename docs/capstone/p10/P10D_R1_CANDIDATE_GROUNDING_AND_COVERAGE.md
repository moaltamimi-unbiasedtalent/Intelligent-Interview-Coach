## 2. Existing behaviour found
`retrieve_evidence()` withheld narrative evidence for a named occupation that matched nothing; `answer()` did not (L-ANSWER-GUARD). The existing guard also **over-blocked**: it treated every leftover resolver phrase as a named occupation, so 14 of 48 retrieval-evaluation questions ("What soft skills are valued across professions?", "SHRM certification", "Is demand for AI skills rising?") were withheld as "unsupported occupations" in the agent path, and 2 more as "ambiguous". Pre-R1 code, measured under the V2 rules (`scripts/p10d_r1_prefix_assessment.py`): of the 386 legacy-sufficient cases, **12 were counted only because the product returned ineligible evidence** (3 synthetic CareerOneStop rows; 9 US BLS handbook and projection rows returned for UK, German and EU "short-term outlook" questions).

## 3. Implemented corrections
1. **One shared grounding step** (`CareerIntelligenceService._apply_occupation_grounding`, called inside `_gather_evidence`, so both paths run it): KNOWN (narrative chunks that are explicitly about a different occupation are withheld; untagged, same-occupation and general chunks stay), AMBIGUOUS (clarification, all narrative withheld; Career Chat now asks instead of answering from nearest neighbours and does not call the model), UNSUPPORTED (a title-like phrase that matched nothing: narrative withheld, the established insufficient-evidence contract), GENERAL (nothing withheld). It uses the resolver outcome and chunk metadata only: no similarity threshold, no new mapping, no P10C resolver. `trace.occupation_grounding` records the decision.
2. **Deterministic occupation-title test** (`evidence_integrity.looks_like_occupation_title`): short phrase, trailing intent nouns removed, no topic, trend, credential or plural-group word, no single-letter token, no bare topic word. After it: 0 of 45 general evaluation questions are withheld as unsupported (1 legitimate ambiguity, "operations manager"). The knowledge-retrieval gate is unchanged (74 of 81, safety 9 of 9, PASS), including the hard unknown-role safety cases.
3. **Provenance classification** (`evidence_integrity`): `verified_real_source` (every recorded origin is real), `synthetic_fixture`, `mixed_origin_unverifiable` (real and synthetic rows share one source id and a row cannot be attributed), `unknown_origin` (no ledger entry: never verified by default). Every evidence item carries, SEPARATELY in `metadata["integrity"]`: source id, country, geography, publication year, edition, origin class, qualification (`not_qualified`: no legacy source is human-qualified) and `source_backed`. No schema change.
4. **Synthetic data at runtime:** evidence from a source whose only origin is a synthetic fixture is now WITHHELD from candidate results (default), with a coverage note; `demonstration_mode=True` (an explicit test or demo) returns it labelled "[SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DATA, not official data]" and "(synthetic sample)". This supersedes the P10D label-only behaviour. Fixtures and history are untouched.
5. **Geographic integrity in the coordinator:** a country-bound item (compensation, forecast, openings, shortage, vacancy, licence, education, training, outlook) tagged to another nation is never returned for a question that names a country (the education and outlook lanes returned the US handbook for UK, German and EU questions). Tags written as names ("Germany"), the store's aggregate label ("European Union - 27 countries") and sub-national jurisdictions ("US-CA") are understood; an EU aggregate is valid only for a European country; unknown geography is not treated as national evidence; language never selects a country.
