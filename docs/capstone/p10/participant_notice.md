# Pilot Participant Notice — ENGINEERING DRAFT

_This is not a legally reviewed research consent form. It is a plain-language notice for a
small capstone usability pilot. The moderator reads/shares it before the session; participation
proceeds only if you are comfortable._

## What this is
- Ask4Mo is a **prototype** ("Intelligent Interview Coach") built for a capstone project.
- The purpose of this session is **usability and product learning** — we are testing the
  product, **not you**. There are no right or wrong answers.
- This is **not a public production service**.

## How it works
- Ask4Mo uses **AI**. **AI can make mistakes** and can be incomplete. Please don't rely on it
  for real decisions during this test.
- Practice **scores and feedback are preparation guidance only** — they do **not** predict or
  decide any hiring outcome, and **no hiring decision is being made** here.
- If you use voice, your **voice is not used to infer** personality, intelligence, honesty,
  confidence, accent or hiring suitability. There is no voice score of any kind.

## Your data
- Please **avoid entering information you would not want used in a test environment.** You can
  use the **synthetic CV and job description we provide** for every core task.
- The moderator may take **anonymous notes** (referred to as "P01", "P02", …). By default we do
  **not** record audio or video. If the owner ever wants to record, they will ask for your
  explicit consent first — recording is **not** required.
- Any observations that are kept will be **summarized anonymously**; your name, email and any
  personal details are **not** stored in the project.

## Voluntary
- Participation is **voluntary**. You may **skip any task** or **stop at any time**, for any
  reason, with no consequences.

## Questions
Ask the moderator anything before you begin. Proceeding with the session indicates you're
comfortable with this notice.
