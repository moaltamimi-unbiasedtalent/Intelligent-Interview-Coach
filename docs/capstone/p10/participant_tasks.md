# Participant Task Sheet (T1–T9)

_Read each task aloud, then observe. Use the supplied synthetic assets in `assets/` — the
participant should not need to enter personal data. Candidate experience only: Platform Admin,
Prompt Lab, RAG diagnostics and reviewer APIs are **not** participant tasks._

Start URL (marketing home): **http://localhost:3015**

---

### T1 — Orient (public Home)
Start on the public home page. **Don't click yet.** Ask the participant:
- "What do you think Ask4Mo is?"
- "Who do you think it's for?"
- "What would you do first?"

Observe their read of the value proposition. Do not teach.

### T2 — Get started
Ask them to **enter the product** (via "Get started" / "Go to your workspace" / sign-in, per the
prepared environment). Observe:
- navigation and where they land (the app home / return journey),
- whether they notice Help / the guided tour.

### T3 — Prepare for a role
Give them the synthetic **job description** (`assets/pilot_job_description.md`). Ask them to
**begin preparing for this role**. Observe:
- where they start,
- whether Mo's response (if available) is **scannable**,
- whether they notice **Brief / Detailed / Show more**,
- whether they can find **Sources**,
- what they think the **next step** is.
> If Mo's answer shows "unavailable" (no LLM key configured), note it as an **environment
> limitation** and focus on the surrounding UX (layout, controls, where they'd look).

### T4 — Document / evidence
Give them the synthetic **CV** (`assets/pilot_cv.md`). Ask them to **upload it** and **review the
extracted evidence**, then:
- **accept** one item,
- **correct** one item,
- **reject** one item.
Then look at the **Story Bank / evidence**. Observe whether **provenance/status** makes sense
(what's verified, what's unreviewed, what happens to a rejected item).

### T5 — Interview Practice
Ask them to **start Interview Practice** and complete enough to experience:
question → answer → feedback → next question. They may type (or use turn-based voice if the
environment supports it). Observe:
- how they **submit**,
- whether they **understand the feedback**,
- how they **interpret the score**,
- what they think the **next action** is.

### T6 — Return journey
Have them navigate away and come back (or sign out/in per the environment). Ask them to find:
- **History**, **Progress**, a **previous report**, and **resume** where applicable.

### T7 — Privacy / trust
Ask them to work out (using Trust/Privacy pages as needed):
- what data is **private**,
- whether a **workspace member automatically sees their CV** (expected answer: no),
- how to **revoke sharing**,
- where the **export / delete** controls are.

### T8 — Voice (if supported)
If the browser/environment supports Web Speech:
- use **Listen**, use **Speak**, **edit** the transcript, then **explicitly submit**.
If realtime is disabled/unconfigured, observe whether the **fallback / unavailable** experience
is understandable. Do **not** mark the pilot failed because an unvalidated external provider is
off.

### T9 — Final exploration
Ask: **"If you came back tomorrow to prepare for another interview, what would you do?"**
Observe whether their mental model matches the product.

---

## Debrief (ask only AFTER they've used the UI — don't coach the answer)
Trust / AI comprehension:
- What did you think was **generated by AI**?
- What information did you believe came from **external sources**?
- Anywhere you were **unsure whether information was verified**?
- Did you understand what Ask4Mo **remembers**, and when something becomes **saved Memory**?
- Did you understand whether **workspace members can see your private data**?
- Was any **score or feedback** unclear?

Response-length UX (we previously shortened Mo's answers on reviewer feedback):
- Was the **first answer enough** to understand what to do?
- Did you know **more detail** was available?
- Did you know where to find the **sources**?

## Optional post-task ratings (1–5, descriptive only; always report n)
Ease of getting started · Clarity of Mo's responses · Usefulness of Practice feedback · Trust in
source/evidence presentation · Confidence personal data stays private · Likelihood of using
Ask4Mo for future interview prep. _(These are pilot ratings, not validated scales.)_
