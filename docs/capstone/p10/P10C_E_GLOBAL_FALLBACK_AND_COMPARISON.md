## 2. Wider South America (GKB-11)
Eleven markets: Argentina, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay, Venezuela (Brazil keeps its P10C-D implementation, unchanged). **No national occupation classification is identified for any of them**, so the only namespace usable today is ISCO (international) with the edition supplied by the source; the profile records only non-data facts: ISO country, ISCO scheme, an ISO 4217 currency EXPECTATION and an ISO 3166-2 subdivision vocabulary to validate region values (Argentina 24, Bolivia 9, Chile 16, Colombia 33, Ecuador 24, Guyana 10, Suriname 10, Uruguay 19, Venezuela 25). **Paraguay and Peru have no vocabulary recorded, on purpose**: regional rows for them are rejected until one is. The currency is not enforced (dollarised and multi-currency economies make a fixed expectation unsafe): the source row must state its own. One reusable family adapter (`south_america_records`) with the same contract as Mexico and Brazil: earnings concept, population and method REQUIRED; household income mappable but refused by the importer and a CHECK; period, currency, population, method, geography and level preserved; a regional row never becomes national. Country source profiles (`data/global_source_candidates.json`, 12 candidates): each national statistics office is a **candidate name only** and only Chile (Encuesta Suplementaria de Ingresos) and Colombia (Gran Encuesta Integrada de Hogares) carry a dataset name (named in planning as national broad examples); every other fact (edition, geography, population, concept, method, currency, period, measure, reference period, publication date, licence, attribution, acquisition) is UNKNOWN. Household surveys may publish individual labour income, household income or both; the concept must come from the publisher.

## 3. ILOSTAT-style international contract (GKB-12)
Nothing about ILOSTAT is held or assumed: no dataset, indicator code, unit, classification label or coverage. `ilostat_records` is a structural contract in which the caller supplies the source's vocabulary as explicit maps: only an indicator mapped to a pay period, a statistic and a non-household earnings concept becomes a record; **employment rates, productivity, GDP and any unmapped indicator are rejected as unsupported statistics** and are never converted to a salary; a non-currency unit (for example a purchasing-power unit) is rejected; the occupation classification and its edition must be stated and mapped (ISCO-88 is a different edition from ISCO-08, never silently merged); ISO3 areas map through a reference table for the named markets only (anything else is rejected). Records are national, harmonised and may reach C3 only through a registry source. No dataset was downloaded or ingested.

## 4. International eligibility, fallback and the precedence boundary (D-16)
The fallback order is exact occupation, then a verified broader group (one hop), always inside the requested country; lower tiers keep their own class (C1, C2, C3), C4 means abstain, and the response carries a categorical `fallback_trace` (stage selected, eligible records per class, `other_country_evidence_used` false). Eligibility precedes precedence (occupation, edition, granularity, country, region, currency, period, basis, statistic, reference period, population, method, approval, qualification); incompatible measurements are a CONFLICT, never ranked as alternatives. The registry vocabulary has no precedence category for a non-Eurostat international harmonised source; the policy (now `p10c-e/1`) therefore carries an explicit configurable boundary `international_families.ilostat` with `enabled: false` (status PENDING OWNER DECISION D-16): a source declaring `international_family=ilostat` in its registry provenance **cannot be imported in production mode**, its evidence is **never operational**, and in internal mode it is flagged `precedence_pending`. Enabling it is a reviewed policy change that this wave does not make. No operational result can depend on the unapproved choice. (The synthetic ILOSTAT-style fixture borrows the `eurostat_harmonised` category only as a fixture stand-in, stated in the corpus.)

## 5. Evidence-state framework (GKB-14; categorical, no confidence percentage)
`assess_evidence_state` is a view over a resolution (it duplicates no qualification authority): SUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, LIMITED_EVIDENCE, AMBIGUOUS, CONFLICTING, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, mapped onto the existing outcome terminology. An EVIDENCE outcome is sufficient only if every factor is strong (C1, exact occupation, detailed granularity, a recorded basis, concept, population and method, an exact or restored official classification, no crosswalk of lesser qualification, no divergent corroboration, no pending precedence decision); otherwise LIMITED with the weak factors named. Factors: source authority, occupation specificity and granularity, geographic specificity, methodological clarity, freshness, classification certainty, crosswalk qualification, completeness, corroboration, precedence decision. The state describes quality, not approval.

## 6. Freshness
Reported as recorded: reference year, reference period, publication date, data vintage, source edition and the registry's retrieval date; `describes_current_market_pay` is always false; a missing date is reported as unknown and never replaced by today's date; whether the reference year is the latest available among eligible evidence is a factor (an older requested year is flagged).

## 7. Conflicts
Comparable same-tier sources that disagree give CONFLICT (preserved with methodology, period and unit, never averaged); incompatible measurements (statistic, period, concept, population, method, currency, year) are preserved separately; precedence orders only comparable evidence (a national figure outranks a comparable international one and the divergence is reported).

## 8. Nominal comparison, FX and PPP
`compare_nominal` compares two resolutions only when currency, pay period, statistic and percentile, reference year, evidence class, occupation specificity and granularity and geographic level all match and the pay basis, earnings concept, population and evidence method are all KNOWN and equal (an unknown matches nothing, even another unknown). The result is the exact Decimal difference (no ratio, no annualisation, no rounding) with caveats (different countries, non-operational evidence) or NOT_COMPARABLE with every reason, or UNAVAILABLE when a side has no evidence. Different currencies are never converted. `compare_fx` and `compare_ppp` always return NOT_CONFIGURED (D-10 pending): no rate or factor is read, fetched, invented or applied and the optional provider argument is never touched (tested with a provider that fails on any access).
