## 3. AI transparency and responsible use (`/ai-transparency`)

Source component: `frontend/components/marketing/AiTransparencyContent.tsx`. Page title: AI transparency and responsible use  `aiTransparency.pageTitle`. Date shown: 2026-09-26.

We aim to be clear about how AI is used in Ask4Mo, in plain language. This page is an engineering draft; the underlying behaviour is described honestly.  `aiTransparency.intro`


#### What Mo is  `aiTransparency.whatMoHeading`

Mo is an AI coaching assistant that helps you prepare. Mo uses a bounded set of tools and follows a governed workflow; it does not act autonomously beyond preparing with you.  `aiTransparency.whatMoBody`


#### When AI is used versus deterministic logic  `aiTransparency.whenHeading`

- AI (a model) is used for conversational coaching, grounded synthesis, question generation and answer and report evaluation.  `aiTransparency.whenAi`
- Deterministic logic (no model) is used for things that must be exact and injection-safe, for example selecting and ranking your already-approved evidence.  `aiTransparency.whenDeterministic`
- The choice of model per operation is governed by a central server-side policy; your browser can never choose a raw model.  `aiTransparency.whenPolicy`

#### Specialists, evidence and sources  `aiTransparency.specialistsHeading`

- Mo may consult bounded specialists (role and opportunity, evidence, coaching) through an allow-list of tools.  `aiTransparency.specialistsBounded`
- Grounded answers cite their sources. When evidence is missing, Mo abstains rather than inventing facts.  `aiTransparency.specialistsGrounded`

#### Human approvals and control  `aiTransparency.approvalsHeading`

- Saving long-term Memory and important handoffs require your explicit approval.  `aiTransparency.approvalsMemory`
- Your feedback helps us improve; it never automatically modifies the running product.  `aiTransparency.approvalsFeedback`

#### Limitations  `aiTransparency.limitsHeading`

- Models can be wrong; Practice scores are guidance, not a verdict.  `aiTransparency.limitsModels`
- Realtime voice is available only where configured; otherwise turn-based voice and typing are used.  `aiTransparency.limitsRealtime`

#### What Ask4Mo never does  `aiTransparency.neverHeading`

- No inference of emotion, mood, stress, confidence, accent, personality, intelligence, honesty or hiring suitability, from your voice or otherwise.  `aiTransparency.neverInference`
- No hiring decisions and no recruiter-facing candidate ranking.  `aiTransparency.neverHiring`
- No exposure of system prompts, developer prompts, chain-of-thought or secrets.  `aiTransparency.neverPrompts`
