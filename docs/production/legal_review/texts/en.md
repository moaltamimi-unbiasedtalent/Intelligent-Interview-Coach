## 1. Privacy (`/privacy`)

Source component: `frontend/components/marketing/PrivacyContent.tsx`. Page title: Privacy  `privacy.pageTitle`. Date shown: 2026-09-26.

This page describes how Ask4Mo handles your data, based on the product's actual technical data inventory. It is an engineering draft and requires legal review; it is not a certification of compliance with any regulation.  `privacy.intro`


#### What we store  `privacy.storeHeading`

- **Account data**: email, display name, verification status, sign-in method, plan tier and platform role.  `privacy.storeAccountTerm` + `privacy.storeAccountDesc`
- **Preparation data**: your goals, conversations with Mo and generated preparation context.  `privacy.storePrepTerm` + `privacy.storePrepDesc`
- **Private documents**: files you upload (for example a CV) stored privately under a random key, never a public URL. OCR-extracted text and detected claims.  `privacy.storeDocsTerm` + `privacy.storeDocsDesc`
- **Interview Practice**: your answers, evaluations and reports.  `privacy.storePracticeTerm` + `privacy.storePracticeDesc`
- **Memory**: long-term preparation facts you have explicitly approved.  `privacy.storeMemoryTerm` + `privacy.storeMemoryDesc`
- **Story Bank**: reusable evidence-backed stories you create.  `privacy.storeStoryTerm` + `privacy.storeStoryDesc`
- **Workspaces and sharing**: workspaces you own or join, and the specific items you explicitly share.  `privacy.storeWorkspacesTerm` + `privacy.storeWorkspacesDesc`
- **Feedback**: ratings and comments you submit on responses.  `privacy.storeFeedbackTerm` + `privacy.storeFeedbackDesc`
- **Operational metadata**: request identifiers, timestamps, coarse event categories and audit records of privileged or security-relevant actions.  `privacy.storeMetadataTerm` + `privacy.storeMetadataDesc`

#### Voice, speech and realtime  `privacy.voiceHeading`

- Turn-based voice uses your browser or operating-system speech engines. Ask4Mo does not record or store your microphone audio.  `privacy.voiceTurnBased`
- Realtime voice (where enabled) streams audio to a realtime provider to power the live conversation; that processing follows the provider's own policy. Ask4Mo still stores no audio.  `privacy.voiceRealtime`
- Ask4Mo never uses your voice to infer emotion, personality, confidence, accent or hiring suitability. There is no voice score.  `privacy.voiceNoInference`

#### External processing  `privacy.externalHeading`

- AI features send necessary text (not secrets) to a model provider to generate responses.  `privacy.externalAi`
- Current-market research (optional) may query external job-market sources.  `privacy.externalResearch`
- Email delivery uses a transactional email provider for verification and recovery.  `privacy.externalEmail`
- Long-lived provider keys stay server-side and are never sent to your browser.  `privacy.externalKeys`

#### Retention, export and deletion  `privacy.retentionHeading`

- You can export your data and permanently delete your account and its application-controlled data (documents, private files, Practice history, Memory, Story Bank and sessions).  `privacy.retentionExport`
- Security and audit records are retained and anonymized (the link to your account is removed) rather than deleted, for security review.  `privacy.retentionAudit`
- Historical infrastructure backups follow the hosting provider's retention schedule and are not erased instantly on deletion; they age out on that schedule.  `privacy.retentionBackups`
- Privacy, security and your data rights are always available, on every plan.  `privacy.retentionRights`

#### Contact  `privacy.contactHeading`

For privacy requests, contact the support address configured for your deployment. This draft will be replaced by legally-reviewed copy before public launch.  `privacy.contactBody`

## 3. AI transparency and responsible use (`/ai-transparency`)

Source component: `frontend/components/marketing/AiTransparencyContent.tsx`. Page title: AI transparency and responsible use  `aiTransparency.pageTitle`. Date shown: 2026-09-26.

We aim to be clear about how AI is used in Ask4Mo, in plain language. This page is an engineering draft; the underlying behaviour is described honestly.  `aiTransparency.intro`


#### What Mo is  `aiTransparency.whatMoHeading`

Mo is an AI coaching assistant that helps you prepare. Mo uses a bounded set of tools and follows a governed workflow; it does not act autonomously beyond preparing with you.  `aiTransparency.whatMoBody`


#### When AI is used versus deterministic logic  `aiTransparency.whenHeading`

- AI (a model) is used for conversational coaching, grounded synthesis, question generation and answer and report evaluation.  `aiTransparency.whenAi`
- Deterministic logic (no model) is used for things that must be exact and injection-safe, for example selecting and ranking your already-approved evidence.  `aiTransparency.whenDeterministic`
- The choice of model per operation is governed by a central server-side policy; your browser can never choose a raw model.  `aiTransparency.whenPolicy`

#### Specialists, evidence and sources  `aiTransparency.specialistsHeading`

- Mo may consult bounded specialists (role and opportunity, evidence, coaching) through allow-listed tools with server-side revalidation.  `aiTransparency.specialistsBounded`
- Grounded answers cite their sources. When evidence is missing, Mo abstains rather than inventing facts.  `aiTransparency.specialistsGrounded`

#### Human approvals and control  `aiTransparency.approvalsHeading`

- Saving long-term Memory and important handoffs require your explicit approval.  `aiTransparency.approvalsMemory`
- Your feedback helps us improve; it never automatically modifies the running product.  `aiTransparency.approvalsFeedback`

#### Limitations  `aiTransparency.limitsHeading`

- Models can be wrong; Practice scores are guidance, not a verdict.  `aiTransparency.limitsModels`
- Realtime voice is available only where configured; otherwise turn-based voice and typing are used.  `aiTransparency.limitsRealtime`

#### What Ask4Mo never does  `aiTransparency.neverHeading`

- No inference of emotion, mood, stress, confidence, accent, personality, intelligence, honesty or hiring suitability, from your voice or otherwise.  `aiTransparency.neverInference`
- No hiring decisions and no recruiter-facing candidate ranking.  `aiTransparency.neverHiring`
- No exposure of system prompts, developer prompts, chain-of-thought or secrets.  `aiTransparency.neverPrompts`
