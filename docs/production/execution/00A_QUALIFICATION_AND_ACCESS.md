### Independent review and conflicts

- **REVIEW-A, REVIEW-B and REVIEW-C** are the three historic qualification-account roles in the [P10C human review protocol](../../capstone/p10/P10C_F2A_HUMAN_REVIEW_AND_APPROVAL.md#2-reviewer-requirements-from-the-existing-authority-model-nothing-new). A is the measurement qualification reviewer for digest-bound batches. B is the occupation alias reviewer for individual evidence attestations. C is the product owner or release approver for the separate future runtime decision. C must differ from the relevant measurement reviewer. The older protocol permits A/B overlap. The 00A register requires three verified reviewer accounts, so keep three separate account slots OPEN and require explicit owner reconciliation before any proposed overlap. Those roles supply no 06G rater slots by implication.
- **HELDOUT** should be independent of the detector, prompt and policy authors. Record authorship, corpus custody, per-locale quotas and hash before evaluation. Treat any known prior tuning exposure or contamination as a finding. The [06E security review package](evidence/06E_SECURITY_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md) and OD-HELDOUT-AUTHOR require a fresh independent held-out set and written decisions before thresholds are used.
- **AI-RATERS** are at least three distinct people per language, for 21 minimum locale-specific slots. Cross-locale participation requires evidenced fluency. Each rater signs their own [conflict and independence declaration](../../../evaluations/language_ai/reviewer_declaration.template.json) and receives only the assigned blinded synthetic packet. A rater must not have authored, translated or edited the material and must rate separately without translation, search or AI assistance. Declare relevant relationships, affiliation, outcome-linked pay and financial interests. Conflicts need written owner disposition before participation and cannot be accepted by automation. AI-LEAD coordinates evidence. AI-APPROVER independently reads the results and owns the LG-04/LG-05 decision once appointed. Existing rubric levels are proposals. [06G protocol and thresholds](evidence/06G_HUMAN_EVAL_PROTOCOL.md)
- **Native, legal and security scopes are distinct.** Obtain the relevant independent declaration and qualified signature for each. A native reviewer can sign legal translation only with separate qualification for that legal scope and an explicit appointment. Signed legal opinion, language evidence, release approval and the two-person Admin access decision each have their own evidence.
