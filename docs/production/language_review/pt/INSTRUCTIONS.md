## Who you are and what you are asked

You are a native speaker of Portuguese appointed by the owner (OD-LANG-03) to judge the text Ask4Mo shows to candidates, or (for the legal part) a qualified person appointed under OD-LANG-04. You are asked to judge text, not the product. Do not use a translation tool, search engine or AI assistant on the text; your judgement is the evidence.

## What to judge

1. Correctness: grammar, spelling and meaning are right; nothing is added, lost or reversed compared with the English source.
2. Naturalness: reads like text written by a fluent professional, not like a translation.
3. Register: the form of address (formal or informal) and the level of formality are appropriate for a careers-coaching product and consistent across the product. State which form is used.
4. Terminology: product and career terms are consistent (the same English term always maps to the same word) and use the term that people in the field actually use.
5. Policy-sentence equivalence: each sentence that states a rule (no unsourced salary, no statistic without a source, not source-backed, nothing saved without a yes) says exactly the same rule as the English, no stronger and no weaker.
6. Absence of English leaks: no English word or phrase remains where the language has a normal word (names of the product, a protected slogan and widely used loanwords are acceptable; say which ones you accept).
7. Protected slogan: the phrase "Ask More. Be More." appears exactly as written, never translated, transliterated or re-punctuated, wherever it appears.

## How to mark a string

Use one verdict per string: `OK`, `MINOR` (wording could be better, not wrong), `MAJOR` (wrong, unnatural or inconsistent; must be fixed), `BLOCKER` (changes a rule, legal meaning or the protected slogan; the language must not be offered until fixed). For anything other than `OK`, write the corrected text. Keep placeholders such as `{name}` untouched and in a position that reads naturally. A hint in the table is only a signal: decide for yourself.
